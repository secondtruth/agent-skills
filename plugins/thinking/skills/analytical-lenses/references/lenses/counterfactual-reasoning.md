# Counterfactual Reasoning

## What this lens sees

What the situation would look like in the absence of the thing under examination, and what alternative paths would have looked like. The lens is a discipline against treating the current state as inevitable.

## Key questions

- If this practice / institution / decision did not exist, what would the situation be?
- What was the previous state (if any)? What problem was the current arrangement created to solve?
- What plausible alternatives exist? Which were considered and rejected? Why?
- Which alternatives were never on the table at all – and why not?
- If we are evaluating an effect: would the effect have happened anyway, in absence of the intervention?
- Is the current arrangement load-bearing for something, or merely persistent?

## Characteristic moves

1. **Subtract**: what does the situation look like with the thing removed? Is the world worse, better, or just different?
2. **Substitute**: what does it look like with an alternative in its place? Generate at least two distinct alternatives.
3. **Find the omitted alternatives**: the set of options actually considered is usually a small subset of the option space. What is missing?
4. **Test the counterfactual baseline**: when measuring an effect, ask "compared to what?". The choice of baseline often determines the answer.

## Known blind spots

Counterfactuals are speculative by nature. They are most reliable when the alternative being compared to is a real precedent (this organization did X before, then changed) and least reliable when the alternative is entirely hypothetical. Also: counterfactual reasoning can be used to manufacture either complacency ("imagine if we didn't have this") or dissatisfaction ("imagine if we had that") depending on which alternatives you choose to consider. The discipline is to consider both kinds.

## Output format

For an existing arrangement: a paragraph on the world-without-it. For a proposed change: paragraphs on 2–3 alternative versions of the change. End with the one or two alternatives that deserve serious consideration vs. the rest.
